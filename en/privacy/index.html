Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in SnipKeep (the “App”).

Effective date / Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Information We Handle

The App handles the titles, contents, categories, favorite settings, and creation and update dates of snippets that you enter and save. This information is used to provide the App's features.

The App does not require account registration. In its current implementation, it does not send or store your snippets on a server operated by the developer.

2. Storage and iCloud Sync

Snippets and categories are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available on your device, your data may sync across your devices through Apple's CloudKit, associated with your iCloud account. You can continue to use the App on your device when iCloud is unavailable.

iCloud storage, syncing, security, and retention are subject to Apple's services and your settings. For information about Apple's handling of data, please see Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Clipboard

When you copy a saved snippet, its selected text is written to the operating system's clipboard. Clipboard use and retention are governed by the operating system and your settings. The App does not read or collect clipboard contents in the background.

4. In-App Purchases

Apple's StoreKit and the App Store are used to purchase SnipKeep Pro, check purchase status, and restore purchases. Apple processes payment information; the developer does not receive your credit card number or similar payment details. Purchases, refunds, and related conditions are subject to Apple's terms and procedures.

5. Use and Disclosure

The App uses the information described above to provide its features, including saving, searching, editing, and copying snippets, syncing across devices, and checking purchase status. Except as required by law, the developer does not sell or provide your snippets to third parties. When you use Apple's iCloud or the App Store, Apple handles information as necessary to provide those services.

6. Retention and Deletion

Saved data remains on your device until you delete it in the App. When iCloud sync is enabled, deletions are propagated to your other devices through Apple's sync process. Please refer to Apple for information about device and iCloud backups and Apple's data retention and deletion practices.

7. Security and Minors

The developer takes reasonable measures to help prevent unauthorized access and disclosure. However, the security of devices, networks, and cloud services cannot be guaranteed. If you are a minor, please use the App with parental or guardian consent where appropriate.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised in response to changes in the App or applicable laws. We will announce material changes on this page or in the App. The revised Policy will take effect when posted on this page.
